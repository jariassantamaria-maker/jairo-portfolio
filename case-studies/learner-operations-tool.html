01 / WHY IT MATTERED

Data existed.
Finding it took work.

Daily active-learner data included business units, assigned advisors, regions, leaders and contact details. Navigating extracts made routine lookup, batch reviews and discrepancy detection unnecessarily difficult.

I wanted operations staff to understand the records and act on them without having to decipher every spreadsheet column or rely on personal interpretation.

02 / METHOD

From ambiguity
to action.

  1. 01

    Extract and organize

    Prepare daily active-learner and assignment data for structured lookup.

  2. 02

    Build usable tools

    Provide search by learner, Business Unit or LA code, bulk cards, list extraction, copy and CSV export.

  3. 03

    Reveal quality gaps

    Show unmatched entries and missing LA, leader or region fields rather than silently omitting them.

  4. 04

    Enable the next action

    Expose contact shortcuts and a data-update request form so discrepancies could be reported for review.

03 / WHAT I BUILT

An interface built
for daily decisions.

The operational widget included Search, Bulk Tools, Analytics and Data QA sections, learner cards and distribution views by company, LA and region.

A batch example displayed 27 searched, 26 found and a rounded 96% match rate. That is one illustrated lookup result, not a sustained system success rate or organizational KPI.

Buttons supported copying information, opening WhatsApp contact and submitting requests to correct inaccurate records. These are interface capabilities, not evidence that every reported issue was resolved.

04 / SCOPE & EVIDENCE

Specific work.
Specific claims.

I built the interactive tool from extracts and used it to surface anomalies and support evidence-based escalations. Production scale, adoption counts, system integrations and realized savings are not independently verified.

The interface and report examples described here are historical work, but the original internal data and screenshots are not reproduced. No measured time savings, retention lift, error reduction or company-wide rollout is asserted.
