01 / THE CHALLENGE

Make reporting
less dependent.

Recurring performance reporting relied on spreadsheet calculations and manual preparation. Important reporting logic depended on individual knowledge and availability.

I proposed moving that logic into a shared reporting view. The aim was to support a more consistent review process and reduce dependence on manual construction of the report.

I translated that operational need into functional requirements and a proposed validation and rollout sequence.

02 / PROJECT ARTIFACTS

Make the
requirements explicit.

  1. 01

    Functional brief

    Describe the reporting need, intended users and continuity concern.

  2. 02

    Calculation definitions

    Specify the performance measures and ranking rules that a reporting view should reproduce.

  3. 03

    Filters & refresh

    Explain the regional review context and recurring update requirements.

  4. 04

    Validation & rollout

    Document logic review, parallel comparison and a subsequent go-live decision.

Public descriptions of deliverables. Internal records, client details and original system materials are not reproduced here.

03 / THE DOCUMENTED PLAN

Plan the checks
before wider change.

The proposal called for reviewing the existing calculation logic, comparing new outputs with the established report in parallel, and then deciding on a wider rollout.

These are stages of the documented plan. The confirmed pilot test does not establish that the full validation and rollout sequence was completed.
