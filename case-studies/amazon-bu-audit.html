CASE STUDY 11 / CROSS-SOURCE AUDITS AND RISK PREVENTION

Spot the mismatch.
Prevent the scramble.

Reconciling Amazon center records before inconsistent structures created avoidable operational emergencies.

01 / WHY IT MATTERED

Multiple sources.
Different answers.

Amazon Career Choice Business Units appeared in administrative records, fulfillment center reference documents and learner organizational associations. A center could exist in one source but be missing or classified differently in another.

Instead of waiting for escalations, I compared the sources, independently investigated discrepancies and documented findings in a format that leaders could understand and act upon.

02 / METHOD

From ambiguity
to action.

  1. 01

    Detect proactively

    Identify differences between Business Unit lists and learner/organization mappings.

  2. 02

    Investigate across sources

    Compare Admin Center data, FC records and, where useful, external location references.

  3. 03

    Classify the anomaly

    Document the Business Unit, missing source, issue type and recommended next action.

  4. 04

    Follow up on changes

    Escalate with evidence, track the outcome and review process improvements.

03 / WHAT I BUILT

A report that
proposes action.

My BU Anomalies Report included the relevant business unit, its presence in FC documentation, the administrative source, anomaly type, recommended action and fields assessing learner membership.

The analysis made individual discrepancies suitable for systematic review and repeatable correction rather than emergency improvisation.

This project reflects the broader end-to-end investigation approach I brought to operational improvement.

04 / SCOPE & EVIDENCE

Specific work.
Specific claims.

I prepared cross-system reconciliations, actionable reports and escalations, and reviewed changes within my scope. The complete number of corrected Business Units and any quantified reduction in incidents are not claimed.

The interface and report examples described here are historical work, but the original internal data and screenshots are not reproduced. No measured time savings, retention lift, error reduction or company-wide rollout is asserted.
