01 / THE CHALLENGE

Make the next
step visible.

Enrollment workflows depended on fragmented instructions, regional exceptions and repeated manual entry. Employees needed to remember which route applied, which checks were required and how to document the request.

I proposed an interactive wizard with conditional routing, step-by-step guidance, review checkpoints and standardized operational notes. The deliverables included functional requirements, process flows and interface concepts for evaluation.

The work remained a design proposal. This prototype demonstrates the interaction using invented rules; it is not evidence of production deployment, adoption or measured business results.

GUIDED EXECUTION

Scope & outcome

The original work was a design proposal, with functional requirements, process flows and interface concepts. This new browser prototype uses fictional rules to demonstrate the interaction. No original production implementation, adoption, time savings or reduction in errors is claimed.
