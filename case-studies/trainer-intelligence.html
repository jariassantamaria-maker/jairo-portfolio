CASE STUDY 10 / DATA STANDARDIZATION AND VISUALIZATION

Normalize first.
Recommend better.

Cleaning inconsistent instructor data so people could find relevant trainers by language, manager and location.

Implemented operational work · Scope qualified

Based on projects and responsibilities performed at goFLUENT. Public narrative only: no internal screenshots, learner or employee records, or unverified business outcomes.

My role
Data cleaning, indexing and interface design
Methods & tools
Advanced Google Sheets formulas, Apps Script and visual dashboards
Deliverables
Language index, filterable trainer directory and world/region maps
01 Challenge02 Approach03 Deliverables04 Scope

01 / WHY IT MATTERED

The right option
was hard to find.

Trainer profiles contained inconsistent language information and location fields. I developed formulas to extract and standardize language capabilities and then created searchable and visual ways to navigate the information.

I wanted recommendations to reflect the learner's actual needs. A mismatch or communication barrier should be investigated before labeling a learner disengaged.

02 / METHOD

From ambiguity
to action.

  1. 01

    Clean the fields

    Review language and location entries for inconsistencies and missing values.

  2. 02

    Index the attributes

    Use advanced Sheets functions including LET, FILTER, SEARCH and TEXTJOIN to identify relevant languages.

  3. 03

    Make them searchable

    Create filters for target language, manager and trainer profile attributes.

  4. 04

    Visualize capacity

    Provide world and regional maps with country-level trainer counts for easier exploration.

03 / WHAT I BUILT

Human-readable
workforce data.

The resulting interface included Search Directory, Split by Language and Interactive Map views, with cards displaying instructor languages, responsible manager and location.

The cleaned index supported informed learner-instructor recommendations. The intended benefit was better fit and engagement, not an independently measured improvement in retention.

The directory and visual maps converted data maintenance into a practical resource for operational users.

04 / SCOPE & EVIDENCE

Specific work.
Specific claims.

I performed cleaning, formula development and interactive dashboard work. The screenshots show interactive views, but do not establish a measured retention increase, an automated matching algorithm or rollout to every team.

The interface and report examples described here are historical work, but the original internal data and screenshots are not reproduced. No measured time savings, retention lift, error reduction or company-wide rollout is asserted.
