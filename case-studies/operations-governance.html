CASE STUDY 08 / OPERATIONAL GOVERNANCE

Control the change.
Keep the context.

Replacing scattered, informal requests with traceable steps that teams could understand and follow.

Implemented operational work · Scope qualified

Based on projects and responsibilities performed at goFLUENT. Public narrative only: no internal screenshots, learner or employee records, or unverified business outcomes.

My role
Process owner & operational coordinator
Methods & tools

Deliverables
Request workflow, confirmation alerts, documentation and assignment reference records
01 Challenge02 Approach03 Deliverables04 Scope

01 / WHY IT MATTERED

A process without
a clear checkpoint.

Requests for CloudTalk and learner assignment updates arrived through different channels. The same information could exist across several documents, and Team Leaders needed a reliable way to request and review updates.

I introduced a structured approach to business proposals and standard operating procedures within my team, with more explicit evidence, metrics, instructions and follow-up than the existing informal practices.

02 / METHOD

From ambiguity
to action.

  1. 01

    Define the request

    Separate proposed changes from validated reference information and shared operational records.

  2. 02

    Create the checkpoint

    Build button-driven email confirmations so no change moved forward without the required acknowledgement.

  3. 03

    Document the rules

    Write SOPs covering required fields, request channels, schedules and exceptions.

  4. 04

    Review and improve

    Follow up with stakeholders, check changes and refine the procedure despite initial resistance.

03 / WHAT I BUILT

A repeatable
request workflow.

Organized three purpose-specific resources: Team Leader proposals, a leader reference repository and shared operational information. The records included LA codes, shifts, home-office days, regions, responsible leaders and assigned communication numbers.

Coordinated CloudTalk phone-resource requests and supported learner distribution reviews with Team Leaders to identify uneven regional workloads.

The process used automated email confirmation notices; this is a concrete workflow automation, not a claim of building a full enterprise approval platform.

04 / SCOPE & EVIDENCE

Specific work.
Specific claims.

I designed the request workflow, wrote supporting SOPs and monitored implementation within my operational scope. Final approvals and organization-wide policy ownership are not claimed.

The interface and report examples described here are historical work, but the original internal data and screenshots are not reproduced. No measured time savings, retention lift, error reduction or company-wide rollout is asserted.
