01 / THE CHALLENGE

Keep the work
understandable.

Daily tracker updates, new requests and weekly reports required different sources and checks. During an absence, a colleague needed a clear way to continue.

I documented the coverage workflow, distinguishing daily learner tracking from weekly activity reporting. The guide explained priorities, source preparation, assignment checks, reconciliation and escalation routes.

Task and handover notes made completed work, blockers and approval dependencies visible.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Document the work.
Preserve the checks.

  1. 01

    Separate the sources

    Identify daily and weekly inputs, their reporting period and the reference data that should remain unchanged.

  2. 02

    Explain the sequence

    Document preparation, updates and checks so the next colleague can follow the reporting routine.

  3. 03

    Name the exception

    Record what is unresolved and route it to the responsible team or authorized approver.

  4. 04

    Leave a next action

    Keep status, blocker and next step together so pending work can be continued.

03 / A FICTIONAL HANDOVER REVIEW

What still
needs attention?

Static illustration · Fictional scenarios

These checkpoints are a new portfolio example. Every source, record, status and responsible role is fictional.

  1. 01

    Confirm the inputs

    A sample weekly file has an unconfirmed reporting period. Next action: confirm the period before using it.

  2. 02

    Validate the report

    One fictional assignment is unmatched. Next action: hold that record for review and reconcile totals with the corresponding source.

  3. 03

    Route the exception

    A calculation question needs a data-review owner; a separate decision needs an approver. Next action: record the evidence and identify each responsible role.

  4. 04

    Leave a usable note

    The fictional report is marked “Waiting for review.” Its note identifies the blocker, owner and next action.

04 / SCOPE & OUTCOME

A documented
way to continue.

The contribution was a handover workflow and task documentation for learning operations. Coverage performance and business outcomes are not measured in this public case.

The illustration above is not an internal system or an actual handover result.
