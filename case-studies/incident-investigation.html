01 / THE CHALLENGE

Understand the
discrepancy.

Recorded attendance statuses and connection activity could appear inconsistent when sessions moved between platforms. Reviewing a discrepancy meant examining both the sequence of events and the limits of each source.

I reviewed lesson logs, connection timestamps and durations, session metadata and operational notes. I compared activity across platforms, documented potential procedural deviations and possible attendance misclassification, and prepared evidence-based recommendations for validation and escalation.

The investigation provided documented findings for review. A connection attempt alone does not establish attendance, completion or responsibility, and implementation of the recommendations is not claimed.

02 / THE ANALYTICAL APPROACH

Build the sequence.
Test the reading.

Make each finding traceable.
Keep the remaining questions visible.

    Frame the discrepancy

  2. 02

    Reconstruct the sequence

    Compare timestamps, durations and relevant notes across the available sources.

  3. 03

    Separate fact from interpretation

    State what the records show, what might explain the gap and what remains unresolved.

  4. 04

    Document the review

    Recommend the additional checks or escalation needed before reconsidering a classification.

What this example can and cannot establish
  • The logs record connection events in the fictional sources. They do not establish the full experience of either participant.
  • The handover note records a request to use Platform B. It does not confirm that the instruction was received or understood.
  • In a real review, timestamps, source coverage, session identifiers and the applicable attendance definition would need validation.
  • The demo never corrects a status, sends an escalation or assigns responsibility. The CSV contains all six fictional events, regardless of the selected sources.

04 / WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

Make the finding
defensible.

EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

Show the source.

Connect a finding to the record that supports it. A reference makes a conclusion easier to review and challenge.

CAREFUL JUDGMENT

Keep the limits visible.

Separate observed activity from a possible explanation. Missing evidence and recorded absence are different things.

ACTIONABLE DOCUMENTATION

Name the next check.

Make unresolved questions and recommended validation steps explicit so the review can move forward.

Scope & outcome

Investigation and reporting were completed. Findings and recommendations supported review of operational discrepancies; implementation is not independently confirmed.
