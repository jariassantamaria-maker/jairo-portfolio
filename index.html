HOW I WORK

Evidence before assumptions.
Action after clarity.

The same practical method connects the investigations, dashboards and SOPs throughout my portfolio.

  1. 01 / DETECT

    Listen & observe

    Identify friction independently through daily operations and first-hand user feedback.

  2. 02 / VERIFY

    Test the evidence

    Compare source records, timelines, technical logs and trends before defining a problem.

  3. 03 / DEFINE

    Make next steps clear

    Document findings, business requirements, process owners, SOPs and proposed actions.

  4. 04 / FOLLOW THROUGH

    Measure & improve

    Coordinate escalations, review implementation where involved and refine the process.
